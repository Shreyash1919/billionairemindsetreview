What the program claims, and how to read it

The sales presentation tells the story of a man named Dave Mitchell and a neuroscientist, and it frames two brainwave states: beta, described as a stressed "slave wave," and theta, described as the "billionaire brain wave" linked to creativity and intuition. It says a bigger hippocampus produces more theta, and that listening daily targets this.

What I'd want a buyer to understand

Theta activity and brainwave entrainment are real topics in neuroscience, and relaxation audio genuinely helps some people wind down or focus. What has not been established is the leap from listening to an audio track to attracting money or changing your financial situation.

The reference list at the bottom of the seller's page is worth reading yourself. Those citations are mostly studies about socioeconomic status and brain development, stress physiology, and neuroplasticity in meditation. They are real papers, but they study populations and conditions, not this audio track, and they do not test whether listening to a sound file changes anyone's income.

So the sensible way to approach it: treat it as a relaxation and mindset audio you might enjoy, judge it on how you feel using it, and use the 90-day guarantee if it isn't for you. Do not buy it expecting money to arrive.
